# Supplementary-Material-Union-Conference-2026-Poster
Supplementary material for "Central nervous system exposure of the novel antituberculosis drug quabodepistat in a rabbit model of tuberculous meningitis" (Union World Conference on Lung Health 2026, poster EP11-742-18): NONMEM code, parameter estimates and diagnostic plots.

Rabbits with symptomatic tuberculous meningitis received oral quabodepistat (2 mg/kg once daily for 3 days). Plasma, CSF, CNS tissues and extra-CNS tissues were collected and analysed with nonlinear mixed-effects modelling in NONMEM. Plasma PK followed a one-compartment model with sequential zero- and first-order absorption and first-order elimination. CSF and tissue penetration were estimated as tissue-to-plasma ratios with plasma–site equilibration.
